### B.2 Right of withdrawal

In accordance with Article L.221-18 of the French Consumer Code, the consumer Buyer has a period of **fourteen (14) days** from receipt of the goods to exercise their right of withdrawal, without having to justify the decision and without incurring costs other than those provided in Articles L.221-23 to L.221-25.

**Exercise of the right**: the Buyer notifies their withdrawal decision to the Partner Brand before expiry of the deadline, either using the standard form annexed to these Terms (Annex 1) or by any unambiguous declaration expressing their intention to withdraw.

**Effects**: the Brand reimburses to the Buyer all sums paid (product + initial delivery fees) at the latest within **fourteen (14) days** of receipt of the withdrawal notification, using the same payment method as the initial transaction.

**Return fees**: unless the Brand has previously announced otherwise, the direct costs of returning the goods are borne by the Buyer.

**Exceptions**: in accordance with Article L.221-28 of the French Consumer Code, the right of withdrawal **cannot be exercised** for products:

- Manufactured according to the Buyer's specifications or clearly personalised;
- Liable to deteriorate or expire rapidly;
- Inseparable from other items after delivery;
- For which the seal has been opened and which cannot be returned for hygiene or health protection reasons.

The vast majority of products listed on Terrassea (custom or semi-customisable outdoor furniture — fabric configurator, dimensions, finishes), **the custom-made exception frequently applies**. This exclusion is mentioned explicitly on the product page before any order.

### B.3 Legal warranty of conformity

In accordance with Articles L.217-3 and following of the French Consumer Code, the Brand is required to deliver goods conforming to the contract and is liable for non-conformity defects existing on the date of delivery. The warranty applies for **two (2) years** from delivery of the goods.

During this period, any non-conformity defect appearing is presumed to have existed on the date of delivery (presumption of anteriority, Article L.217-7 of the French Consumer Code), unless proof to the contrary is provided by the Brand.

**Remedies**: the consumer Buyer chooses between **repair** or **replacement** of the goods, subject to the cost conditions provided in Article L.217-9 of the French Consumer Code. Failing that, the Buyer may obtain a price reduction or contract termination as provided in Articles L.217-14 to L.217-17.

Costs incurred for compliance (shipping, labour, materials) are borne by the Brand.

### B.4 Hidden defects warranty

Independently of the legal warranty of conformity, the Buyer may choose to act on the basis of the hidden defects warranty provided in Articles 1641 to 1649 of the French Civil Code. The Buyer may then choose between contract termination or a price reduction, in accordance with Article 1644 of the French Civil Code.

This action must be brought within a period of **two (2) years** from discovery of the defect.

### B.5 Optional commercial warranty

The Brand may offer a complementary commercial warranty (e.g. « 5 years aluminium structure »). This commercial warranty:

- Does not under any circumstances release the Brand from its obligations under legal warranties.
